# Demo Guide (3-5 minutes)

**Input used throughout:** *"I know Canva + AI tools, have 2-3 hours/day and want to earn in USD."*

**Before you start:** run `streamlit run app.py`. With a key in `.env` you get live Gemini output; without one, the app shows a **Demo / Fallback Mode** banner and the same flow works. For a predictable stage demo, tick **Demo mode** in the sidebar (AI settings).

**Opening line (10 s):** "New freelancers know a skill but not who to sell to. HustlePilot takes them from skill to a reviewed, personalised first message, and never sends anything for them."

| # | Step (page) | Do | Say |
|---|-------------|----|-----|
| 1 | **My Profile** | Click *Load the demo example* | "This is the Canva + AI tools person with 2-3 hours a day." |
| 2 | **My Profile** | Click *Analyze profile* | "Honest read of strengths, limits and what to build first." |
| 3 | **Discover Niches** | *Generate niche options* | "Four options with trade-offs. No 'best' is declared." |
| 4 | **Discover Niches** | *Select this niche* (e.g. dental and aesthetic clinics) | "The user chooses." |
| 5 | **Validate Niche** | *Validate niche* | "Left: AI assumptions. Right: how to verify them. We never invent statistics." |
| 6 | **Build Offer** | *Build offer*, open the Packages tab | "Three packages, labelled Suggested Example Pricing, not market data." |
| 7 | **Ideal Client** | *Generate ideal client profile* | "Who exactly to look for, and the signals that show fit." |
| 8 | **Prospects** | Open the page | "19 fictional demo prospects, clearly labelled. Not live leads." |
| 9 | **Prospects** | *Qualify prospects*, open the Qualified tab, filter by country | "Each one scored against the ICP, with why and what problem we can help with." |
| 10 | **Client Sample** | Pick a top prospect | "Now a specific one." |
| 11 | **Client Sample** | *Generate sample idea* | "A post, reel idea and caption to show value first. Labelled AI-generated, never claimed as delivered." |
| 12 | **Outreach** | *Generate campaign* | "Short, human, personalised email plus two follow-ups." |
| 13 | **Outreach** | Edit a line, press *Approve this campaign* | "Human in the loop. Copy and email actions only unlock after approval." |
| 14 | **Outreach** | Press *Mark as contacted* | "I send it myself, then record it." |
| 15 | **Follow-ups** | Show the schedule and the *Due now* area | "Follow-ups are tracked; statuses are editable." |
| 16 | **Dashboard** | Open the page | "Pipeline: total, qualified, contacted, follow-ups due, interested, closed." |

**Tip for showing "Follow-up Due":** on Follow-ups, set a lead's date to today via *Schedule follow-up date*. The lead moves to *Follow-up Due*.

**Closing line (10 s):** "No guaranteed clients, no guaranteed income, no auto-sending. A clear path, with the user in control."

**If something goes wrong on stage:** tick *Demo mode* in the sidebar and press the button again. Every step has a built-in fallback.
